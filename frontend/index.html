    Data provenance

    • DEM: Gale Crater pilot 1 km², 198×200 cells (dx 5.003 m, dy 4.997 m), row 0 = north.
    • Optical: MRO CTX mosaic, 200×201 px, ~5 m/px, same bounding box.
    • Thermal: 2001 Mars Odyssey THEMIS IR Band 9 (12.57 µm), I01350002PBT, night 03:15 LMST, 10×10 valid 100 m pixels (−9999 border removed, never used as a temperature).
    • Bounds: 137.5195–137.5365° E, −4.7010 to −4.6842° N.

    Profile limits (rover 20°, astronaut 25°) and cost weights, including the thermal cold-trap penalty, are experimental demo settings — not NASA-certified traversability or EVA safety limits. Brightness temperature is not contact temperature.
